Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Temple Story ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application "Temple Story" (the "App").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully to understand our practices regarding your personal data.

📱 Quick Summary

  • We collect: Location data (only when you enable tracking) and app usage data
  • We don't collect: Personal identifiers, contact information, or sell your data
  • Your control: You can disable location tracking at any time
  • Data storage: All temple visit data is stored locally on your device

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Location Information (Foreground Only)

When you enable location tracking in the App, we collect your device's location to detect temple visits. This is a foreground-only feature, meaning:

  • Location tracking only works when the App is open and visible on your screen
  • We do NOT collect location data when the App is closed or running in the background
  • You can enable or disable location tracking at any time
  • Location data is processed on your device and is NOT transmitted to our servers

2.2 Temple Visit Data

When you visit a temple (detected by proximity), we record:

  • Temple name and location
  • Visit duration (minimum 5 minutes required)
  • Date and time of visit

All temple visit data is stored locally on your device only. We do not upload or transmit this data to any servers.

2.3 Favorites and User Preferences

If you mark temples as favorites, this preference is stored locally on your device. We do not collect or transmit this information.

2.4 Device Information

We may collect basic device information such as:

  • Device type (Android/iOS)
  • Operating system version
  • App version

This information is used solely for app functionality and improvement purposes.

3. Permissions We Request

Permission Purpose Required?

🔒 Important Privacy Notes

  • We DO NOT sell or share your personal information
  • We DO NOT use your data for advertising purposes
  • We DO NOT require account creation or personal information

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for:

  • Detecting and recording your temple visits
  • Displaying nearby temples on the map
  • Generating visit certificates
  • Providing app functionality (favorites, visited temples list)
  • Improving app performance and user experience

5. Data Storage and Security

5.1 Local Storage

All your personal data, including temple visits, favorites, and preferences, is stored locally on your device using secure storage mechanisms provided by your operating system.

5.2 No Cloud Storage

We do not upload, store, or process your personal data on any cloud servers. All data remains on your device at all times.

5.3 Temple Database

Temple locations and information are sourced from OpenStreetMap (OSM) and are publicly available. This data does not contain any personal information.

6. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

6.1 OpenStreetMap (OSM)

Temple locations and map data are sourced from OpenStreetMap, which is open-source and publicly available. OSM's data is licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL).

6.2 Google Play Services

If you download the App from Google Play Store, Google may collect certain information as described in their Privacy Policy.

7. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Disable Location Tracking: You can turn off location tracking at any time from the app's main screen
  • Revoke Permissions: You can revoke location or photo permissions from your device settings
  • Delete Local Data: You can clear app data from your device settings, which will remove all temple visits and favorites
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling the app will remove all locally stored data

8. Children's Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. We encourage parents and guardians to monitor their children's use of our App.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

10. Google Play Store Compliance

10.1 Location Permission Declaration

Temple Story requests foreground-only location permission to detect when users visit temples. This is a core feature of the app and is essential for its primary functionality.

10.2 Prominent Disclosure

This app collects location data to enable temple visit detection while the app is open and in use.

Location tracking features:

  • ✅ Foreground-only - Works only when the app is open and visible
  • ✅ User-controlled - Enable/disable at any time from app settings
  • ✅ Device-local - Location data is processed on your device and NOT transmitted to servers
  • ✅ Optional - You can use the app without enabling location tracking

We do NOT collect location data when:

  • ❌ The app is closed
  • ❌ The app is running in the background
  • ❌ Location tracking is disabled by the user

10.3 Primary Functionality

Location access is essential to the app's core functionality of detecting and recording temple visits. Without location access, the app cannot determine when you are near a temple.

10.4 Background Location

This app does NOT request, collect, or use background location data. Location tracking is automatically disabled when the app is closed or running in the background. We only use foreground location APIs.

10.5 Permissions Requested
