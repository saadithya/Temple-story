How It Works

1

Enable Location

Turn on location tracking in the app. We only track when the app is open.

2

Visit a Temple

Go near a temple (within 100m) and stay for 5-10 minutes while the app is open.

3

Visit Recorded

Your visit is automatically saved to your device. View it anytime in the Visited tab.

4

Get Certificate

Export a beautiful certificate showing your temple visits. Save or share with pride!

🔒 Your Privacy Matters

Temple Story is designed with your privacy in mind. We use foreground-only location tracking, meaning we only access your location when the app is open and visible. All your temple visit data is stored locally on your device. We never collect, upload, or share your personal information.
